# Kazakh and English terminology study

## Status

Not yet run. No participant evidence exists in this repository. Do not mark requirement 7 complete until the results table below is filled from real sessions.

## Participants

Recruit 6–8 current IELTS learners: at least three who prefer Kazakh and three who prefer English, spanning self-reported bands 5–7. Obtain consent and do not collect names, recordings, or full practice answers for this terminology study.

## Tasks (15–20 minutes)

1. Show the result screen in the participant’s preferred language. Ask: “What does ‘estimated practice band’ mean? Is it an official IELTS score?”
2. Ask the participant to identify the four assessed criteria and open the official descriptor source.
3. Ask what the system can and cannot judge reliably from text alone and from audio.
4. Ask the participant to stop future history storage, delete the current audio, and delete saved writing.
5. Show a rate-limit state and ask when they would retry and whether their draft is lost.
6. Show two attempts and ask which criteria improved or declined.
7. Switch language and ask whether the paired Kazakh/English terms mean the same thing.

## Terms to probe

| English | Kazakh shown in product | Comprehension target |
|---|---|---|
| Estimated practice band | Болжамды practice band | A practice estimate, not an official result |
| Fluency and coherence | Еркін сөйлеу мен байланыстылық | Flow and logical connection in speech |
| Coherence and cohesion | Байланыстылық пен құрылым | Organization and linking in writing |
| Lexical resource | Лексикалық қор | Range and appropriate use of vocabulary |
| Grammatical range and accuracy | Грамматика мен дәлдік | Variety and correctness of structures |
| Pronunciation | Айтылым | Intelligibility, stress, rhythm and sounds |
| Data retention | Деректерді сақтау | What is saved, where, and for how long |
| Delete | Өшіру | Irreversible removal of the selected data |

## Success criteria

- At least 5/6 participants explain without prompting that the band is not official.
- At least 5/6 correctly identify the text/audio boundary.
- Every participant completes all three privacy actions without facilitator help.
- At least 80% correct comprehension for each paired term; revise any weaker term and retest it with three new learners.
